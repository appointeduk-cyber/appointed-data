Questions

Where does the information come from?

Public records only: The Gazette, Companies House, and official sources such as the Bank of England and GOV.UK. See how we work.

Do you sell the assets or take a fee?

No. We're a publisher. We don't value assets, arrange sales or take commission. Every deal lists the office holder to contact directly.

What is the Distress Index?

One number for UK company insolvencies over the last four weeks, against the 2024 to 2025 average (100). It's built from every Gazette notice, counted once per company, and checked every month against the official statistics. See it live.

Is this investment advice?

No. It's information compiled from public records. Always do your own checks with the office holder.
