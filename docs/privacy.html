Privacy

Your data

Short version: we collect your email address to send you Appointed, and nothing else without asking.

What we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your email address. When you open or click our emails, our email provider records that, so we can see which parts of the newsletter are useful.

Why

To send you the newsletter you asked for, and to improve it. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give when you subscribe and can withdraw at any time.

Who handles it

Our newsletter is sent through beehiiv, which stores subscriber details on our behalf. We don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies of its own. The sign-up box is provided by beehiiv.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe in one click from any email. You can also ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you by emailing appointeduk@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Public records in the newsletter

The companies we write about come from public records. We don't publish the names or personal details of directors, shareholders or other individuals.
